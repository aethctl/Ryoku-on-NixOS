LATEST // CURRENT NIXOS PORT

0.60.1
BETA.19

RYOKU 0.60.1-BETA.19
NIXOS PORT

A broad parity release focused on bringing the 0.60.1 Ryoku surface across cleanly while keeping NixOS-specific ownership, persistence and packaging intact.

PARALLAX

Declarative wallpaper depth.

Parallax integration is packaged and delivered through the NixOS port rather than depending on Arch-style mutable installation paths.

DISPLAY

Stronger monitor persistence.

Per-monitor behavior and HDMI persistence were hardened for the NixOS environment.

THEMING

More system surfaces follow Ryoku.

KDE palette integration, spectrum retints, and visualizer presentation were brought into the parity pass.

DESKTOP

Preserve user-owned state.

Ghostty and other user-facing configuration paths were handled with stronger preservation instead of blind replacement.

POWER

Power profiles integrated.

NixOS-specific integration keeps power behavior part of the declarative host rather than an external mutable setup step.

RYOTUNES

Native NixOS packaging.

Ryotunes is packaged with its daemon/frontend runtime exposed correctly to the user service environment.

ARCHIVE // 0.59 SERIES

0.59
SERIES

HISTORICAL
NIXOS PARITY PASS

The 0.59 work established the first broad parity baseline and proved that the Ryoku desktop could be delivered as a coherent NixOS module/package set rather than a pile of mutable compatibility scripts.

PARITY

Desktop audit completed.

The major visible and runtime surfaces were checked against upstream and moved into the Nix delivery model.

HUB

Plugins and key sounds.

Hub plugin surfaces and key-sound support were included in the NixOS parity work.

VIRTUALISATION

Ryoport / Looking Glass.

Optional Ryoport and Looking Glass support was packaged without forcing passthrough or VFIO onto the host.

FOUNDATION

Nix-native ownership.

The port settled on normal NixOS generations, flake inputs and explicit package/module ownership as its foundation.
